## 🐱 A Very Large Kitty!

"ROOOOOOOAR!" Colton nearly jumped out of his sandals.

"WHAT WAS THAT?" Grayson grabbed Tumbles, their fluffy orange kitten, and held him tight.

"That," whispered Hadley, "was a lion."

The three children were imagining themselves visiting ancient Persia, where they could watch a Bible adventure unfold without being seen or changing anything that happened. And today, they were about to discover some very big cats.

"How big?" asked Grayson. Hadley stretched out her arms.

"Bigger than you!"

"Bigger than ME?" exclaimed Colton.

Tumbles wriggled out of Grayson's arms and began chasing a butterfly.

"He doesn't seem worried," said Hadley. "That's because he hasn't seen the lion yet!" answered Grayson.

But the lions weren't the biggest danger in this story. The real trouble was happening in the king's palace.

## 👑 The King's Favorite Helper

King Darius had an important man working for him. His name was Daniel. Daniel was honest. He worked carefully. He could be trusted. And he loved God.

In fact, Daniel did his job so well that the king planned to put him in charge of the whole kingdom!

"Wow!" said Grayson. "That's a BIG job!"

"Lots of people to organize," agreed Hadley. "And lots of buildings!" added Grayson.

Colton pretended to stand on a palace tower. "ATTENTION, EVERYBODY! TIME FOR LUNCH!" Hadley giggled. "I don't think that's exactly what Daniel did."

But not everybody liked Daniel. Some of the other leaders were jealous. They searched for something dishonest Daniel had done. They looked. And looked. And looked some more. But they couldn't find anything!

Finally, one of them had a sneaky idea. "We'll have to find something against him concerning the law of his God." And they made a terrible plan.

## 📜 The Sneaky New Law

The jealous leaders went to King Darius. "O king, live forever!" They told him they wanted a special law. For thirty days, nobody could pray to any god or person except the king. Anyone who disobeyed would be thrown into a den of lions.

"LIONS?" whispered Grayson. Colton frowned. "That's not fair!"

Hadley shook her head. "They know Daniel prays to God. They're trying to trap him."

The king agreed to the law and signed it. And under the law of the Medes and Persians, the decree couldn't simply be changed.

The jealous leaders smiled. Their trap was ready.

## 🙏 Three Times a Day

Daniel heard about the new law. Did he stop praying? Did he hide his faith? Did he decide that thirty days wasn't very long?

No. Daniel went home, just as he usually did. He went upstairs to his room, where the windows were open toward Jerusalem. And he knelt down and prayed to God.

Just as he had always done. Three times a day.

Hadley watched quietly. "He's not trying to show off," she whispered. "He's just continuing to worship God."

Grayson nodded. "But he knows he'll get in trouble." Colton stopped bouncing. "He's really brave."

Tumbles curled up beside Hadley and began to purr. *Prrrrrrrrr.*

Meanwhile, the jealous leaders were watching Daniel's house. They saw him praying!

"AHA!" they cried. And they hurried away to tell the king.

## 😟 A King Who Couldn't Fix His Mistake

When King Darius heard their accusation, he was terribly upset. He liked Daniel!

All day long, the king tried to find a way to save him. But the leaders reminded him of the law he had signed.

Finally, Daniel was brought to the lions' den. King Darius said to him: "Thy God whom thou servest continually, he will deliver thee."

Then Daniel was lowered into the den. A huge stone was rolled over its opening. The king sealed it. And Daniel was left with the lions.

Grayson looked at Hadley. "Can we build a rescue machine?"

"A rope ladder!" suggested Colton. "Or a giant crane!" said Grayson. Hadley shook her head sadly. "No one can rescue him that way now."

The children watched the dark den in their imaginary adventure, unseen by the people around them. Then they heard something.

A low, rumbling sound. *Grrrrrrrrrrrr.* Tumbles' ears stood straight up.

## 🌙 The Most Astonishing Night

Inside the den were enormous lions. Their paws were bigger than Tumbles. Their teeth were sharp. Their claws were powerful.

But something extraordinary had happened. **God had sent His angel to shut the lions' mouths!** The lions couldn't hurt Daniel!

Grayson stared in amazement. "Those lions could swallow Tumbles in one bite!" Colton counted the lions he could see. "One… two… three…" "Don't get too close!" whispered Hadley.

Of course, the children were only watching the Bible adventure in their imaginations. They couldn't touch the lions or interfere with Daniel.

Tumbles, however, had discovered that his own tail was following him. He turned. His tail turned. He spun. His tail spun. He spun faster. **WHOOPS!** He toppled over onto his back.

Colton covered his mouth to keep from laughing. "He's the silliest little lion I've ever seen!"

But Hadley was thinking about Daniel. "He must have been so grateful to know God was with him."

Above the den, King Darius wasn't having nearly such a peaceful night. The king returned to his palace. He didn't eat. He didn't want entertainment. And he couldn't sleep. He was worrying about Daniel.

## 🌅 A Very Happy Morning!

As soon as morning came, King Darius hurried to the lions' den. He called out in a worried voice: "O Daniel, servant of the living God, is thy God, whom thou servest continually, able to deliver thee from the lions?"

For one moment, there was silence. Then a voice answered from inside the den! "O king, live forever!"

**IT WAS DANIEL!** Daniel explained that God had sent His angel and shut the lions' mouths. He was unharmed!

The king was overjoyed. He ordered Daniel to be lifted out of the den. And when they looked him over— Not one scratch! Not one bite! Not one injury!

Grayson threw both hands into the air. "HE'S ALIVE!" Colton jumped as high as he could. "HOORAY!" Hadley smiled so widely that her cheeks hurt.

Tumbles gave a squeaky little— "MEEEEOW!" It was almost as if he were celebrating too.

The king dealt with Daniel's accusers and sent out a proclamation honoring Daniel's God, the living God whose kingdom would never be destroyed. And Daniel continued serving faithfully.

## 🐾 The Little Lion Who Wasn't So Brave

As their imaginary adventure came to an end, Tumbles spotted a tiny beetle climbing over a stone. He crouched. His whiskers twitched. He took one brave step forward.

The beetle suddenly spread its wings. **BUZZZZ!** Tumbles leaped backward! Straight into Grayson's arms!

"Whoa!" laughed Grayson. "You weren't afraid of the lions, but you're scared of a BEETLE?" Colton giggled. "Maybe Tumbles needs courage lessons!"

Hadley was still thinking about Daniel. "You know, Daniel didn't suddenly become faithful when he was thrown into the lions' den."

"What do you mean?" asked Grayson. "He had already been praying to God every day. He was honest at work. He obeyed God even before there was any danger."

Grayson thought about that. "So Daniel had practiced being faithful?"

"Exactly," said Hadley. "And when the really hard day came, he kept trusting God."

Colton smiled. "Maybe we can practice too." "How?" asked Grayson. "By telling the truth," said Hadley. "By praying. By doing what's right, even when it's hard." "And by not being scared of beetles!" added Colton.

Tumbles gave an offended little meow. All three children burst out laughing.

And somewhere in ancient Persia, Daniel's story reminded them that courage isn't just about facing big, frightening lions. Sometimes, courage begins with the small choices we make every day.

## ❤️ This Week's Spiritual Lesson

**Faithfulness in the little things prepares us to trust God in the big things.** Daniel didn't wait for trouble to begin praying. His friendship with God was already part of his daily life. When the new law threatened him, he continued doing what was right.

God delivered Daniel miraculously, but faithfulness doesn't guarantee that we will always escape danger. We can trust God whatever happens, because He never stops caring for His children.

**Memory verse — Daniel 6:22 (KJV)**

> "My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lions' mouths, that they have not hurt me."

**Something to think about:** If Daniel had waited until he was in trouble to begin praying, how might his story have been different? What's one small way you can practice being faithful to God this week?
