## 🐕 A Rather Unusual Parade

“SQUIRREL!”

Ella suddenly bounded down the dusty road, her fluffy white ears bouncing with every step.

“Ella! Come back!” shouted Colton, leaping over a rock.

Grayson was busy building a little tower of stones beside the road, while Hadley sat beneath an olive tree, singing a song she had made up.

Tumbles, their little orange kitten, was attempting to catch a grasshopper.

**BOING!**

The grasshopper jumped.

**POUNCE!**

Tumbles landed on Grayson's tower.

**CRASH!**

“TUMBLES! That took me FOREVER to build!”

Just then, two men came walking down the road toward the village of Bethphage.

“Hey!” said Hadley. “Those are two of Jesus' disciples!”

The children scrambled to their feet.

“Where are you going?” asked Colton.

“Jesus has sent us to fetch a young donkey,” one of them explained.

“A donkey?” asked Grayson. “What does Jesus need a donkey for?”

The disciples smiled and continued toward the village.

Naturally, the children followed.

## 🫏 The Donkey Nobody Had Ridden

Just as Jesus had told His disciples, they found a young donkey tied up in the village.

As they began untying it, its owners asked why they were doing so.

“The Lord hath need of him,” the disciples answered.

The owners let them take the donkey.

Hadley reached out and gently stroked its nose.

“He's adorable!”

“Almost as adorable as Ella,” said Colton.

The donkey lowered its head to investigate Tumbles.

Tumbles looked up.

The donkey sniffed.

Tumbles sniffed.

Then the donkey sneezed.

**AH-CHOOOO!**

Tumbles jumped straight into Grayson's arms.

“It's just a donkey!” laughed Grayson.

The disciples led the donkey back to Jesus and placed their garments on its back. Then Jesus sat upon it.

“Has anyone ever ridden that donkey before?” Hadley asked.

One of the disciples shook his head.

“Never.”

The children watched in amazement as Jesus began riding toward Jerusalem.

## 🌿 A Parade Without a Trumpet

As they traveled down the Mount of Olives, more and more people gathered.

Some took off their outer garments and spread them on the road. Others cut branches from trees.

People were shouting joyfully and praising God for the wonderful miracles Jesus had performed.

“**HOSANNA!**”

“**BLESSED IS THE KING THAT COMETH IN THE NAME OF THE LORD!**”

Hadley began singing along with the crowd.

Grayson found a fallen branch and waved it enthusiastically.

Colton scrambled onto a large rock so he could see over everyone's heads.

Ella wagged her tail so hard that her entire back end wiggled.

Meanwhile, Tumbles had discovered a beautiful red garment lying beside the road.

He crawled underneath it.

Then he crawled through one of its sleeves.

Then he tried to turn around.

That was a mistake.

“MEOW!”

Grayson rushed over and rescued him.

“You're supposed to be celebrating Jesus, not getting dressed for the parade!”

Hadley burst out laughing.

## 👑 Where Is the King's Crown?

As the procession continued, Grayson looked thoughtfully at Jesus.

“Where's His crown?”

“And His soldiers?” asked Colton.

“And His beautiful royal clothes?” added Hadley.

They had heard stories about kings who rode magnificent horses, wore expensive robes, and had servants everywhere.

But here was Jesus, riding a borrowed donkey.

An older man standing nearby heard their conversation.

“Long ago,” he explained, “the prophet Zechariah said that Jerusalem's King would come riding humbly upon a donkey.”

Hadley's eyes grew wide.

“So this was supposed to happen?”

The man nodded.

The children looked at Jesus again.

He didn't need a golden crown to be a King.

He didn't need a mighty army to show His greatness.

He was the King whom God had promised.

## 🪨 The Rocks That Almost Sang

Not everyone was enjoying the celebration.

Some Pharisees called out to Jesus, telling Him to rebuke His disciples and make them stop praising Him.

Jesus answered:

> “I tell you that, if these should hold their peace, the stones would immediately cry out.”

Colton looked down at the rock beneath his feet.

Then he jumped off it.

“Did you hear that?!”

Grayson picked up a little stone and held it close to his ear.

Nothing.

Hadley giggled.

“Maybe that one's a soprano.”

Grayson pointed toward an enormous boulder.

“And THAT one's definitely a bass!”

Hadley pretended to conduct an orchestra of rocks.

Ella tilted her head.

Tumbles sat on a pebble.

The children laughed, but Hadley understood something wonderful about what Jesus had said.

Jesus was so worthy of praise that even the stones would cry out if everyone became silent.

So she began singing again.

## 🧺 A Different Kind of Greatness

Later that afternoon, the children found Aunt Angie sitting beneath a shady olive tree with a basket of freshly baked flatbread, grapes, and other delicious food.

Uncle Jonathan was helping her unpack everything.

Ella immediately lay down beside Angie, hoping for both a snack and a snuggle.

“Did you see Jesus?” Angie asked.

“YES!” all three children answered.

“He rode a donkey!” said Colton.

“And people were praising Him everywhere!” said Hadley.

“And He didn't even need a palace or a fancy horse!” added Grayson.

Just then, the children noticed a little boy nearby struggling to carry a heavy basket.

Grayson jumped up.

“I can help him!”

Colton hurried after him and picked up some of the things that had fallen out.

Hadley offered the boy some grapes.

Aunt Angie smiled.

“You know, Jesus taught us that being great isn't about having everyone serve us. It's about serving others.”

Grayson thought about that.

“So you don't have to be a king to do something great?”

“That's right,” said Uncle Jonathan.

Tumbles climbed into the now-empty breadbasket, curled himself into a little orange ball, and began to purr.

Colton laughed.

“Look! We have a king after all!”

“King Tumbles the First!” announced Grayson.

“King of the Breadbasket!” added Hadley.

And everyone laughed.

## ❤️ This week's spiritual lesson

**True greatness is found in humility and serving others.**

Jesus is the King of kings, yet He entered Jerusalem riding a humble donkey. He didn't need expensive clothes, a magnificent horse, or a golden crown to prove His importance.

When we help someone, share what we have, or put someone else's needs before our own, we are following the example of Jesus.

**Memory verse — Matthew 23:11 (KJV)**

> But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.

**Something to think about:** What could you do today to make someone else's day happier without expecting a reward?
