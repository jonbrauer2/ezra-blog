Once upon a time, in the beautiful state of Colorado, where the mountains stretched high into the sky, there lived two little twin boys who were about to have a VERY important day.

Tomorrow was their BIRTHDAY! 🎂🎈🎉

All week long, the twins had been wondering what their birthday might bring.

Would there be cake?

Definitely.

Would there be presents?

Probably.

Would there be wild running, jumping, wrestling, laughing, and somebody getting turned upside down?

Well… there was a very good chance of that.

Because someone special was coming to visit.

Far, far away, Uncle Jonathan climbed onto an airplane.

He flew across the country…

Over cities.

Over fields.

Over rivers.

And finally toward the great big Rocky Mountains.

And the whole way there, Uncle Jonathan could hardly wait.

Because waiting for him in Colorado were two birthday boys and their awesome big sister!

When Uncle Jonathan finally arrived, there were hugs all around.

But the hugs lasted approximately three seconds.

Because then—

“GET HIM!”

The twins ATTACKED!

“AAAAAAAH!” cried Uncle Jonathan as two little boys came charging across the room.

One grabbed an arm.

One grabbed a leg.

And their big sister jumped into the action too.

Suddenly Uncle Jonathan was trapped underneath a gigantic pile of giggling children.

“Oh no!” he shouted. “I have been defeated!”

But then…

Uncle Jonathan opened one eye.

“Actually…”

The children froze.

“…I think it’s time for the UNCLE JONATHAN TICKLE ATTACK!”

“NOOOOOOOOO!”

Everyone scattered.

Feet thundered through the house.

Around the couch they went.

Down the hallway.

Behind the chairs.

Up onto the cushions.

There was wrestling.

There was tackling.

There were pillow battles.

There were children being scooped up and carried around like sacks of potatoes.

And every few minutes someone would yell:

“DOGPILE ON UNCLE JONATHAN!”

WHUMP!

Down he went again.

But that was only the beginning of their Colorado adventure.

There were so many things still to do.

They could explore outside beneath the enormous Colorado sky. They could go looking for adventures, play games, tell ridiculous jokes, eat birthday treats, stay up just a little later than usual, and invent competitions that made absolutely no sense.

Who could jump the farthest?

Who could make the weirdest face?

Who could build the biggest pillow fort?

And, most importantly…

Could all three kids wrestle Uncle Jonathan to the ground at the same time?

The twins were quite certain the answer was YES.

Uncle Jonathan was quite certain the answer was ABSOLUTELY NOT.

Their big sister knew the truth:

With her help, Uncle Jonathan didn’t stand a chance.

And then tomorrow morning, two little boys would wake up and realize—

IT WAS FINALLY THEIR BIRTHDAY!

There would be celebrating and laughing and birthday surprises.

But the very best part wasn’t the presents or the cake.

It was that their uncle had gotten on an airplane and flown all the way across the country because there was nowhere he’d rather be than right there in Colorado, celebrating his two birthday boys and spending time with their big sister too.

And when the weekend was over, Uncle Jonathan knew he would fly home with tired arms from wrestling, sore cheeks from laughing, and a heart completely full.

Although there was one thing he was slightly worried about.

Because somewhere in that Colorado house…

three children were whispering.

They were making a plan.

A very sneaky plan.

And Uncle Jonathan heard one of the twins say:

“Tomorrow morning…”

Then their big sister whispered:

“…we jump on him.”

And the other twin giggled.

“Birthday dogpile.”

Uncle Jonathan pulled the blanket over his head.

Tomorrow was going to be a very long day.

And he couldn’t wait. ❤️

The End.
