“Did you hear that?” whispered Hadley.

Grayson stopped stacking little wooden blocks.

Colton stopped pretending his sleeping mat was a trampoline.

Tumbles stopped washing his paw.

Everyone listened.

**Scratch. Scratch. Scratch.**

“It's a lion!” Colton whispered.

Grayson looked down.

It was Tumbles scratching his ear.

“Very small lion,” he said.

Uncle Jonathan smiled. “Tonight's Bible adventure is about someone who heard a voice in the night.”

In their imagination, the children traveled to Shiloh, where the people of Israel came to worship God. They could watch what happened without changing the story.

A boy named Samuel lived there, helping an elderly priest named Eli.

Samuel's mother, Hannah, had prayed for a child. When God answered her prayer, she kept her promise to dedicate Samuel to the Lord's service.

Now Samuel was growing up, learning to serve God.

And tonight, he was about to learn something very important.

---

## Someone Is Calling

It was nighttime.

Eli had gone to bed. His eyesight had grown very weak.

Samuel was lying down, too. The lamp of God had not yet gone out.

The sanctuary was quiet.

Then came a voice.

“Samuel!”

Samuel sat up.

“Here I am!”

He hurried to Eli.

“Here I am, for you called me.”

But Eli said, “I did not call. Lie down again.”

Samuel returned to his sleeping place.

Colton looked puzzled.

“Maybe Eli talks in his sleep.”

“Maybe,” Grayson whispered.

Hadley tilted her head. She loved listening to music, and she knew how important it was to listen carefully to a sound.

“Let's find out.”

Tumbles climbed onto Colton's lap.

Colton whispered, “If somebody calls YOU, don't wake everybody up.”

---

## Again? And Again?

“Samuel!”

Samuel got up again.

He went straight to Eli.

“Here I am, for you called me.”

Eli said, “I did not call, my son. Lie down again.”

Samuel obeyed.

Grayson counted on his fingers.

“That's twice.”

The Bible tells us that Samuel did not yet know the Lord in the way he soon would. God's word had not yet been revealed to him.

Then the Lord called a third time.

“Samuel!”

Up went Samuel!

Off he went to Eli.

“Here I am, for you called me.”

Colton held up three fingers.

“THREE trips! That's a lot of getting out of bed.”

This time, Eli realized what was happening.

It was the Lord calling the boy.

Eli told Samuel to go back and lie down. If the voice called again, Samuel should answer, “Speak, LORD; for thy servant heareth.”

Hadley smiled.

“He doesn't have to run anywhere this time.”

Grayson nodded. “He needs to listen.”

---

## Speak, Lord

Samuel returned to his place.

Then the Lord came and stood and called as before:

“Samuel, Samuel!”

This time Samuel answered, “Speak; for thy servant heareth.”

The children grew very quiet.

Even Tumbles seemed to understand that this was no time for kitten gymnastics.

God gave Samuel a serious message about Eli's family. Eli's sons had been doing very wrong things, and Eli had failed to restrain them. God told Samuel that their wrongdoing would have consequences.

It wasn't an easy message to hear.

The next morning, Samuel opened the doors of the house of the Lord. He was afraid to tell Eli about what God had said.

“Would you be afraid?” Hadley asked.

Colton nodded.

Grayson nodded, too.

Eli asked Samuel to tell him everything.

And Samuel did.

He didn't leave out the difficult parts or change the message to make it nicer.

Eli answered that it was the Lord, and He should do what seemed good to Him.

As Samuel grew, God was with him. All Israel came to recognize that Samuel was a true prophet of the Lord.

---

## Listening With Our Hearts

Back beside Uncle Jonathan, Hadley thought for a moment.

“Samuel was really good at listening.”

“Eventually,” Grayson said. “At first he thought it was Eli.”

“That's right,” Uncle Jonathan said. “He needed someone to help him understand. And when he understood, he was willing to listen and obey.”

Colton looked down at Tumbles, who was trying to fit inside a very small basket.

“Does God talk to kittens?”

“I'm not sure Tumbles would sit still long enough,” Grayson said.

The kitten's tail stuck out of the basket.

Hadley giggled.

“Can we listen to God, too?”

“Absolutely,” Uncle Jonathan said. “We can read the Bible, pray, and ask God to help us understand and do what He teaches. We don't have to wait for a voice in the night. God has already given us His Word.”

“And even if He tells us something difficult?” Grayson asked.

“Especially then.”

Colton gently lifted Tumbles out of the basket.

“Come on, kitty. Time to practice listening.”

Tumbles immediately ran in the opposite direction.

“Well,” said Hadley, “one of us needs more practice.”

And everyone laughed.

---

**Something to remember:** Listening to God means more than hearing His words. It means being willing to understand, trust, and obey Him—even when doing so is difficult.

**Memory verse:** “Speak; for thy servant heareth.” — 1 Samuel 3:10 (KJV)
