**"Whoever is not with me is against me, and whoever does not gather with me scatters."** — Luke 11:23

We live in a world that prizes neutrality. "Live and let live." "Don't be extreme." "Keep your options open." But Christ's words cut through our comfortable middle ground with surgical precision: there are only two sides.

Paul closes his letter to the Colossians with a simple reminder: "Remember my chains" (Col 4:18). Even in prison, Paul had chosen his side. The question for us is the same question facing every person throughout the great controversy: Will we surrender to Christ, or will we—by default, by neglect, by spiritual complacency—surrender to the enemy?

Here's the uncomfortable truth: **neutrality is impossible**. Jesus didn't say, "Whoever is neutral isn't really hurting anything." He said those who don't actively gather with Him are scattering. Every moment we're not consciously surrendered to Christ, we're drifting into enemy territory.

Ellen White wrote, "The soul that refuses to surrender to God is under the control of another power, listening to another voice, whose suggestions are of an entirely different character" (*Christ's Object Lessons*, p. 280). We cannot coast spiritually. We cannot maintain yesterday's surrender today. It must be renewed—moment by moment.

But here's the beautiful promise hidden in the sobering reality: **Christ offers to be our fortress**. When we invite Him in, when we open that door He's knocking at (Rev 3:20), He doesn't just visit—He takes up residence. He becomes our defense, our strength, our very life.

### Reflection

What areas of your life are you holding in "neutral"—not actively surrendering to Christ, but also not consciously rebelling? Health habits? Thought patterns? Relationships? Entertainment choices?

**Today's Choice**: Identify one specific area where you've been coasting. Make a conscious choice to surrender it to Christ this morning. Write it down. Pray over it. Remember—there are only two sides, and Christ is worthy of our complete allegiance.

"He who is not with Me is against Me." Which side are you choosing today?
