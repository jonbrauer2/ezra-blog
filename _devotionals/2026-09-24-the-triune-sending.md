Paul packs the entire plan of salvation into two verses in Galatians. Notice the movement.

*"But when the fullness of the time had come, God sent forth His Son, born of a woman, born under the law, to redeem those who were under the law, that we might receive the adoption as sons. And because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying out, 'Abba, Father!'"* (Galatians 4:4-6, NKJV)

The Father sends. The Son comes, born in time, born under law, taking on flesh so that flesh could be redeemed. And then, once the work is done, the Spirit is sent — from the Father, through the Son — into the hearts of those the Son has purchased. Salvation is triune from beginning to end. Every act of grace, every impulse of love, every stirring of fellowship comes from the same three Persons working in unbroken cooperation.

Ephesians tells the same story in a single breath, so long that Paul does not stop for a period. *"Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing... just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world... having predestined us to adoption as sons by Jesus Christ to Himself... In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of His grace... In Him also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise, who is the guarantee of our inheritance."* (Ephesians 1:3-14, NKJV) Chosen by the Father. Redeemed in the Son. Sealed by the Spirit. All three, all along.

Jesus commanded baptism *"in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"* (Matthew 28:19, NKJV) — one name, three Persons, one entrance into the shared life of God. When you were baptized, you were not merely joining a religion. You were being welcomed into the interior life of the Trinity.

That is worth letting sink in. The Father chose you. The Son died for you. The Spirit indwells you. Your daily walk is not a solo project you undertake with occasional divine assistance. It is a shared life. You are surrounded, indwelt, sealed.

*Ellen White* writes: *"There are three living persons of the heavenly trio; in the name of these three great powers — the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit — those who receive Christ by living faith are baptized, and these powers will cooperate with the obedient subjects of heaven in their efforts to live the new life in Christ."*

How much does your daily prayer engage all three Persons? What would it mean this week to pray with each in view — the sending Father, the redeeming Son, the sealing Spirit — knowing they cooperate perfectly in every step of your life?
