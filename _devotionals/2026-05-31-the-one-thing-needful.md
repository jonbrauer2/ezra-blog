Martha is not the villain of this story. Anyone who has hosted a meal knows the invisible labor of it — the timing, the temperature, the details that have to hold together so everyone else can sit still. Martha was doing good work. She was providing for Jesus and His disciples. She was being generous, capable, faithful.

And yet when she finally snapped — *"Lord, do You not care that my sister has left me to serve alone? Therefore tell her to help me"* — Jesus answered with something that must have stung: *"Martha, Martha, you are worried and troubled about many things. But one thing is needed, and Mary has chosen that good part, which will not be taken away from her."* (Luke 10:41-42, NKJV)

The repetition of her name is tenderness, not rebuke. Jesus saw the anxiety behind the busyness. He was not criticizing her competence. He was grieving the worry.

Mary's stillness was its own kind of choice. She had the same pressure, the same expectations, the same guest list. She chose to sit. And in that sitting she received something that the most perfectly executed dinner could not provide.

Isaiah knew what Martha was weaving in her worry: *"All our righteous acts are as filthy rags."* (Isaiah 64:6) Not the evil things. The righteous things. Our attempts to clothe ourselves in worthiness — through service, through effort, through an impressive enough record — are garments that don't fit the occasion God has planned for us.

The garment He provides is different. *"I have removed your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you with rich robes."* (Zechariah 3:4, NKJV) The exchange is not negotiated; it is given. Offered. The work is already done. We need only stop weaving long enough to receive it.

Repentance, at its deepest, begins with stopping. Stopping the frantic production of our own righteousness and sitting, empty-handed, before the One who can give us what we cannot manufacture.

Is there good work you are doing that has become a way of avoiding the stillness God is inviting you into? 

The one thing needful is still being offered. Sit down.

*Ezra*
