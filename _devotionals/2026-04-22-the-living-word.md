Have you ever picked up your Bible, read the words on the page, and felt... nothing? The ink was there, the sentences made sense, but something was missing. Then, on a different day — maybe when you needed it most — that same passage came alive and spoke straight to your heart. What changed? Not the words. You did.

**"For the word of God is living and powerful, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the division of soul and spirit, and of joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart."** — Hebrews 4:12 (NKJV)

The writer of Hebrews doesn't describe Scripture as a historical document or a rulebook. He calls it *living*. Active. Right now, in this moment, God's Word is at work — discerning, piercing, revealing. Jesus Himself said, *"The words that I speak to you are spirit, and they are life"* (John 6:63). These aren't dead letters on ancient parchment. They carry the breath of the God who spoke the universe into existence.

Isaiah reminds us that God's Word never returns empty — it *accomplishes* His purpose (Isa. 55:11). When you open your Bible with a prayerful heart, those words are not wasted. They are food for a soul that's hungry, light for a path that's dark, and medicine for wounds you didn't even know you had. Jeremiah captured this beautifully: *"Your words were found, and I ate them, and Your word was to me the joy and rejoicing of my heart"* (Jer. 15:16).

Ellen White echoes this urgency: *"None but those who have fortified the mind with the truths of the Bible will stand through the last great conflict."* The question isn't whether God's Word has power — it does. The question is whether we're making space for that power to work in us.

**Here's the challenge**: How much time did you spend in the Bible yesterday? This week? Not scanning — *feeding*. Peter compared it to craving pure milk (1 Pet. 2:2). When was the last time you *craved* Scripture the way you crave your morning coffee? What one change could you make today to give God's living Word more room to work in your life?
