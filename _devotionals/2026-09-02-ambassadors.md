An ambassador does not deliver an opinion. An ambassador delivers a message. The voice is not the ambassador's own. The words come from a country the ambassador represents. When the ambassador speaks, the sending nation is understood to be speaking.

Paul uses that image for every Christian. *"Now then, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were pleading through you: we implore you on Christ's behalf, be reconciled to God."* (2 Corinthians 5:20, NKJV)

As though God were pleading through you. Sit with that for a moment. The message being delivered through fragile human mouths is a plea from God Himself. He is not asking us to think up something clever to say. He is asking us to carry His voice into rooms He is not visibly present in.

The starting point for the ambassadorship is not our worthiness. It is His finished work. *"God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not imputing their trespasses to them, and has committed to us the word of reconciliation."* (2 Corinthians 5:19, NKJV) The reconciliation is already accomplished. What remains is the announcement. The ambassador does not create the peace treaty. The ambassador tells people that one is on offer.

Paul lays out the exchange in the closing line. *"He made Him who knew no sin to be sin for us, that we might become the righteousness of God in Him."* (2 Corinthians 5:21, NKJV) The one who never sinned was made to bear sin — not sinful, but reckoned as sin in our place. And in the exchange, we become — reckoned as — the righteousness of God. That is the news the ambassador carries. Not: try harder. But: the exchange has already happened; come and receive it.

Which is worth pausing on for a person who feels unqualified to speak for God. You are not being asked to represent your own righteousness. You are being asked to point to His. You are not being asked to be impressive. You are being asked to be honest about what has happened to you. The clay pot from yesterday's reading is the ambassador. The cracks do not disqualify. They authenticate.

*"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new."* (2 Corinthians 5:17, NKJV) Already. Though not yet complete. The new creation is under way in you, and the neighbors who watch you are watching an ambassador of a country that has not yet fully arrived.

Ellen White writes: *"We are Christ's witnesses, and we are not to allow worldly interests to so absorb our time and attention that we pay no heed to the things which God has commanded us to place first."*

You are an ambassador. The voice of a pleading God to at least one person in your life today. Who is it? What would it look like to speak on His behalf, not your own?
