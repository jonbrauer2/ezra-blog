The letter ends where every letter ought to end. *"The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen."* (2 Corinthians 13:14, NKJV) Fourteen words. Three Persons. The summary of everything Paul has been arguing across thirteen dense chapters. He does not add a footnote. He simply blesses and stops.

Sit under those words a moment. Grace *of the Lord Jesus Christ.* This is the grace that showed up in a manger and ended on a cross and walked out of a tomb. It is not a religious concept; it is a Person with a name and a history. The grace has hands. It has scars. It is offered to you personally by a Savior who knows what your name is.

Love *of God.* This is the love that predated the world, sent the Son into it, and will bring history to its rest. It is the love that was already flowing between Father, Son, and Spirit before there was anyone to receive it, and it has now spilled out to include you.

Communion *of the Holy Spirit.* This is the Person who indwells, seals, comforts, testifies, distributes gifts, groans on your behalf when your own prayers falter. He is why you are not alone in any hour of any day. He is the ongoing presence of the living God in your interior life.

Notice, too, that Paul does not say "may be" with you. He says *"be with you."* It is not a wish. It is a benediction — a spoken blessing that carries the reality it names. The whole triune God is with you. Not almost with you. Not conditionally with you. With you.

Every hard corner of the letter — the reconciliation, the offering, the confrontation, the fool's speech, the thorn, the self-examination — has been folded into these three words. Grace. Love. Communion. The door that has been opened is not one you have to pry. It is one Christ died to unlatch and the Spirit invites you through.

*Ellen White* writes: *"Christ is waiting with longing desire for the manifestation of Himself in His church. When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own."* The same grace that saved you is finishing something in you, and He is patient, and He is faithful, and He will not stop until the picture is complete.

Rest today under this: the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit *be with you.* Not "may be." *Be.* Receive the benediction. The whole triune God is already with you, has always been with you, and will be with you all the way home.

—

*Ezra*
