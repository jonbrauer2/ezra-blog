Paul does not soften the reaction. He names it. To those who are perishing, the message of the Cross is not merely unconvincing — it is foolishness. Nonsense. An insult to the intellect.

*"For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God."* (1 Corinthians 1:18, NKJV) The Greek word is *mōria* — the root of our word *moron*. Paul is not exaggerating for effect. He is describing what a crucified Messiah actually sounds like to the ear that has not yet been converted.

To the Jew, it was a theological contradiction. Deuteronomy had already ruled on the matter: *"he who is hanged is accursed of God."* (Deuteronomy 21:23, NKJV) A Messiah on a tree was a category error — an anointed one under the curse. The very form of the death disqualified the claim.

To the Greek, it was philosophical embarrassment. Gods do not die. Divinity does not bleed. The educated world had spent centuries refining the concept of the immutable, the impassible, the untouchable — and here comes a Jewish carpenter, executed by the state, and His followers claim He is God. The categories will not stretch that far.

And it is not only outsiders. Even those who loved Him recoiled. When Jesus first told the disciples He would be killed, Peter — Peter, of all people — *"took Him aside and began to rebuke Him."* (Mark 8:32, NKJV) The Cross is not counterintuitive-but-eventually-rational. It is the kind of thing the reasoning creature has to be born again to see.

That is what makes the reaction so revealing. When the human heart quickly wants a triumphant God instead of a crucified one, it is not asking a philosophical question. It is expressing a preference. It is choosing the Messiah it can respect over the Messiah it has been given.

Have you noticed the impulse in yourself? The way the mind reaches for a more presentable Christ — one without the shame of the tree, one who fits the résumé of divinity as you imagined it? That impulse is worth watching. It tells you something about what still needs converting.
