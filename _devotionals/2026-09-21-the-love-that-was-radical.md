*"God is love."* (1 John 4:8, NKJV) Familiar words. So familiar, in fact, that we have to work to hear them the way the ancient world heard them, because in that world the phrase was closer to a scandal than a slogan. Gods, in the first century, were mostly arbitrary. They were angry. They had to be appeased with grain and wine and animals and, in some cases, worse. To say a god *was* love — that love was the essence, not the reward for good behavior — was a statement so strange it required the entire Cross to make it credible.

John says the demonstration was the Cross. *"In this the love of God was manifested toward us, that God has sent His only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through Him. In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son to be the propitiation for our sins."* (1 John 4:9-10, NKJV) Notice the polarity. Not our love reaching up. His love coming down. The whole movement originates in Him.

Paul says the same thing with the timing as the argument. *"But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."* (Romans 5:8, NKJV) While we were still. Not after we cleaned up. Not once we made it worth His while. Not even when we were sincere seekers. While we were still opponents, still on the run, still building lives out of whatever we could grip — that is when the Cross was raised. The timing was the argument. God did not wait for us to become lovable. He loved us first, and lovable is what the loving is making of us.

And then Paul again: *"But God, who is rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us, even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ."* (Ephesians 2:4-5, NKJV) Dead. Not weak. Not struggling. Dead. Corpses do not participate in their own resuscitation. They simply hear the voice that says *live* and are.

*Ellen White* writes: *"'God is love.' His nature, His law, is love. It ever has been; it ever will be."* Before the world was made, God was love. In the world's future, when every enemy is stilled, God will still be love. Love is not what God does under favorable conditions. It is what God is.

If you were truly loved at your worst, does that change how you love others at theirs? Who in your life is currently in a state that only Cross-love could reach? That person is exactly the invitation.

—

*Ezra*
