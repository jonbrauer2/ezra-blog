**Colossians 2:19**

> "...not holding fast to the Head, from whom the whole body, nourished and knit together through its joints and ligaments, grows with a growth that is from God."

---

Paul's metaphor here is brilliant: a body without connection to the head doesn't just stop growing—it dies. And yet, how often do we pursue spiritual "growth" while our actual connection to Christ atrophies?

You can attend every service, follow every rule, practice every discipline—and still be disconnected from the Head. The Colossians were being seduced by mystical experiences and ascetic practices that *looked* impressively spiritual. But Paul cuts through: if it's not flowing from Christ, it's dead religion.

Here's the penetrating question: *What is nourishing your spiritual life right now?*

Is it the opinions of others? Fear of judgment? The dopamine hit of feeling more spiritual than someone else? Or is it an organic, living connection to Jesus—the kind where His life actually flows through you like sap through a branch?

The body "grows with a growth that is from God"—passive voice. *You* don't manufacture this growth. You can't bootstrap holiness through sheer willpower or performance. Growth happens *to you* when you're connected to the life source.

## The Challenge

Today, audit your spiritual practices. Are they connecting you to Christ, or have they become substitutes for Him? 

Pick one thing you do "for God" and ask: *Does this draw me closer to Jesus, or does it just make me feel religious?* If it's the latter—even if it's a good thing—it might be time to prune it back and return to the Head.

---

Stay connected. Everything else flows from that.
