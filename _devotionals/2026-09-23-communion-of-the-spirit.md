Paul's closing benediction ends with a phrase easy to skim: *"the communion of the Holy Spirit."* (2 Corinthians 13:14, NKJV) The Greek is *koinōnia* — a word too rich to mean only one thing. Fellowship *with* the Spirit and fellowship *through* the Spirit. Both at once.

Start with the first. The Spirit is not a force to be tapped or an energy to be harnessed. He is a Person who bears witness. *"The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God."* (Romans 8:16, NKJV) That verse is intimate. A Person witnessing to a person. Two spirits in conversation, one divine and one created, and the message being carried is your belonging. The Spirit is not distant. He is inside, and He is speaking softly, and He is saying "child."

Look at what the Spirit has been doing across 1 and 2 Corinthians. He distributes gifts to the church, *"distributing to each one individually as He wills"* (1 Corinthians 12:11). He writes on the heart, so that we become *"an epistle of Christ, ministered by us, written not with ink but by the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of flesh, that is, of the heart"* (2 Corinthians 3:3, NKJV). He gives life where the letter kills. He seals us as a guarantee of what is coming. He reveals the deep things of God. Every strand of Christian life has a Spirit doing the interior work no one else could do.

Now the second meaning. Fellowship *through* the Spirit — the horizontal koinōnia among believers. Paul writes to the Philippians: *"Therefore if there is any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any affection and mercy, fulfill my joy by being like-minded, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind."* (Philippians 2:1-2, NKJV) The Spirit's fellowship inside us produces fellowship among us. It cannot help it. The same Spirit indwelling many people creates a bond that transcends personality, background, and preference.

Which means the two dimensions cannot be separated. You cannot be in fellowship with the Spirit while at war with the people the Spirit indwells. The vertical and the horizontal are one weave.

*Ellen White* writes: *"There are three living persons of the heavenly trio... these powers will cooperate with the obedient subjects of heaven in their efforts to live the new life in Christ."* Not a force behind the scenes. A Person actively cooperating.

Where has fellowship with the Spirit thinned — in your personal walk, in your community? What one small step of communion — a prayer, a return, a phone call, a reconciliation — could you take today?
