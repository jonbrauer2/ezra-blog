## 🔑 Central Theme

[Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13)'s second beast, together with the dragon and the sea beast, forms what has been called Satan's *counterfeit trinity* — three malignant powers arrayed against the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This lesson identifies the earth beast, in Adventist prophetic tradition, as the *United States of America*, and traces its role in orchestrating the final crisis: enforcing worship of the first beast, working miracles, creating the "image of the beast," and imposing the *mark of the beast* on those who submit.

The identification of the earth beast as the U.S. rests on four features: (1) it arises *after* 1798, when the sea beast received its wound; (2) it arises from the "earth" — a sparsely settled region, in contrast to the crowded "sea" of ancient nations; (3) it has "two horns like a lamb" — an initially gentle, non-monarchical, non-papal system founded on Christian principles; (4) it eventually "speaks like a dragon" — using its power to enforce the very kind of religious coercion the sea beast pioneered. The United States, Ellen White and Adventist interpreters have argued, uniquely fits this description.

The lesson presses hard on two claims. First, the final crisis is a *worship* crisis, and its distinguishing test will be *the Sabbath* — the fourth commandment being the specific point of loyalty to the Creator, and Sunday observance being the historic symbol of the beast's authority to modify God's law ([Dan. 7:25](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan7.25)). Second, the crisis is not merely institutional but *spiritual* — false miracles, deception through spiritualism, and a global false revival will accompany the beast's rise, so that only those grounded in Scripture will discern the counterfeit.

The lesson also insists on evangelistic charity. Adventism's identification of Sunday-keeping as the historical mark of the beast is a claim about *the sea beast's authority-claim*, not about individual Sunday-keeping Christians who love Jesus. The mark is not yet universally imposed. To say "Sunday is the mark of the beast" without the necessary distinctions is both exegetically imprecise and pastorally destructive.

The Memory Text — "the Lamb standing on Mount Zion with the 144,000" — is Revelation's own answer to Chapter 13. Wherever the beast triumphs on earth, the Lamb has already gathered His people on the heavenly mountain. The end of the story is not in doubt.

## 📚 Key Passages — Exegetical Notes

### [Revelation 13:11](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.11) (Sunday — The Earth Beast Introduced)

- **"Another beast coming up out of the earth":** Greek *allo thērion anabainon ek tēs gēs*. "The earth" — as opposed to "the sea" of v. 1 — is apocalyptic vocabulary for a sparsely populated area, in contrast to the crowded ancient world. [Jer. 2:6](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Jer2.6) uses "the earth" for wilderness; [Rev. 17:15](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev17.15) makes "the sea" the peoples and multitudes. The earth beast rises from open territory.
- **"Two horns like a lamb":** The horns are not crowned — unlike the sea beast (v. 1) — signaling that this power is not autocratic or monarchical. The lamb-likeness suggests gentleness, Christian roots, and initial harmlessness.
- **"He spoke like a dragon":** The tension of the beast's character. Lamb-like in appearance; dragon-like in speech. Founded on Christian principles; eventually wielding coercive religious power. This is the pattern of *religious nation* moving toward *religious empire*.
- **"Coming up out of the earth":** The verb *anabainein* ("come up") echoes [Gen. 1:24](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Gen1.24) — "let the earth bring forth living creatures." The earth beast is *God-permitted* — even, in a sense, *God-birthed*, though it grows into what God never intended.
- **The historical fulfillment:** Adventist interpretation, articulated by Ellen White in *The Great Controversy* (pp. 440–441) at a time when the U.S. was a relatively small power, identifies the earth beast as the United States. The four features fit — arose after 1798 (Constitution ratified 1789; established by 1798); rose in sparsely settled territory; initially non-monarchical and Christian-influenced; but with the potential to speak "as a dragon" through the union of civil and religious power.

### [Revelation 13:12](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.12) (Monday — Union of the Two Beasts)

- **"Exercises all the authority of the first beast in his presence":** Greek *poiei enōpion autou*. The earth beast acts *in the presence* (*enōpion*) of the sea beast — the language of a subordinate operating on behalf of a superior. The earth beast becomes the *enforcement arm* of the sea beast's authority.
- **"Causes the earth and those who dwell in it to worship the first beast":** The verb *poieō* — "to cause, to make" — is causative. The earth beast is not merely persuasive; it *compels*. This is where coercion enters.
- **Ellen White's identification:** "This prediction has been fulfilled in the appearing of the papacy... And the same spirit will be manifested in Protestantism when it obtains control of the civil power" (*Great Controversy* 442). The prediction is not that Catholicism will directly persecute; it is that *Protestantism*, in alliance with civil power, will enforce the sea beast's religious authority.
- **How the union works:** Not by Catholicism absorbing Protestantism, but by Protestantism becoming *like* Catholicism in its use of civil power for religious coercion. The endgame is not a merger; it is a *convergence in method*.

### [Revelation 13:13–14](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.13) (Tuesday — Miracles and Deception)

- **"He performs great signs, so that he even makes fire come down from heaven":** Greek *sēmeia megala*. The word *sēmeion* has been used positively in the Gospels for Christ's works of power; here it is used negatively, of the beast's power to deceive. Signs alone do not authenticate.
- **Fire from heaven — biblical significance:**
  1. **[1 Kings 18:20–39](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.1Kgs18.20) — Elijah on Carmel:** Fire from heaven authenticated the true God against Baal. The earth beast's imitation is a *reversal* — a false claim to the very sign by which Elijah was vindicated.
  2. **[Acts 2:1–4](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Acts2.1) — Pentecost:** The Spirit descended as fire. [Revelation 13:13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.13) may point to a *false outpouring of the Spirit* — a counterfeit revival of worldwide scope.
  3. **[2 Thess. 1:7–8](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.2Thess1.7) — Christ's return:** Christ comes in *flaming fire*. The false miracle may include a counterfeit Second Coming — a demonic imitation of Christ's arrival, deceiving the world.
- **The Adventist reading:** The final deception will involve *spiritualism* — supernatural manifestations that appear to authenticate the beast's authority. This is why the biblical doctrine of *conditional immortality* (the dead sleep, do not communicate) is such an important safeguard. If the dead cannot appear, then supposed communications from deceased saints or "apparitions" of Mary must be diabolical impersonation.
- **The rule for the end:** No sign, however spectacular, authenticates a doctrine that contradicts Scripture. The test is *Scripture*, not *phenomenon*. This is why [Deut. 13:1–5](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Deut13.1) is so essential — a prophet whose sign comes true but who leads to other gods is *not* from the LORD.

### [Revelation 13:14–15](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.14) (Wednesday — The Image of the Beast)

- **"Telling those who dwell on the earth to make an image to the beast":** An image (Greek *eikōn*) is a *representation* that shares the character of what it represents. The earth beast constructs a religio-political system that replicates the sea beast's methods — union of church and state, enforcement of religious orthodoxy by civil law.
- **Parallel with [Daniel 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3):** Nebuchadnezzar built an image on the plain of Dura and commanded worship on pain of death. The three young men refused. The parallel to [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13) is precise:
  - An image (statue in [Dan. 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3); image-system in [Rev. 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13))
  - A worship command (music at Dura; economic-social pressure in [Rev. 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13))
  - A death penalty for refusal (fiery furnace; killing in [Rev. 13:15](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.15))
- **The commandment at stake:** [Daniel 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3) concerned the *second* commandment (no image-worship). [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13), according to Adventist reading, concerns the *fourth* commandment (Sabbath, the sign of the Creator's authority). Both are commandments that Rome, in [Daniel 7:25](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan7.25)'s sense, "thought to change."
- **"Given breath to the image":** Greek *dounai pneuma tē eikoni* — echoing [Gen. 2:7](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Gen2.7), where God breathed life into Adam. The earth beast claims the divine prerogative of *giving life* — sustaining the image-system as if it were alive. This is blasphemous power.
- **"Cause as many as would not worship the image to be killed":** The final crisis includes a death decree against those who refuse the beast's worship. Not all will be martyred (God has other means of preservation), but the *threat* will be universal.

### [Revelation 13:16–18](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.16) (Thursday — The Mark and the Number)

- **"He causes all... to receive a mark on their right hand or on their foreheads":** The Greek *charagma* — "mark, engraving, brand." Not necessarily a literal physical mark. In apocalyptic vocabulary, marks on the forehead or hand indicate *loyalty* — mind (forehead) or work (hand). Compare [Deut. 6:8](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Deut6.8) — "Bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes."
- **Two marks, two loyalties:** [Rev. 14:1](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev14.1) — the 144,000 have the *Lamb's name* on their foreheads. [Rev. 13:16](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.16) — the beast's followers have the beast's mark. Every human is marked by one or the other. There is no neutral zone.
- **Sabbath vs. Sunday as the historical marker:** Adventist reading identifies the mark of the beast with Sunday observance *when enforced by civil law* as the alternative to God's Sabbath. This is not because Sunday-keepers today have "the mark" — the crisis is future — but because Sunday observance is the historic *symbol* of the sea beast's claimed authority to modify God's law ([Dan. 7:25](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan7.25)). When Sunday observance becomes *coerced*, then the mark is received.
- **Sunday-keepers today do NOT have the mark:** The mark is received *when* the crisis is on, *when* the beast's authority is publicly asserted, and *when* the individual chooses that authority over God's clear commandment. Millions of sincere Sunday-keeping Christians love Jesus and will be saved. The point is not their present observance but the *future crisis* when the choice is presented starkly.
- **The number 666:** [Rev. 13:18](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.18) — *hexakosioi hexēkonta hex* — "six hundred sixty-six." Possibilities:
  1. **Vicarius Filii Dei ("Vicar of the Son of God"):** This Latin title, if letters treated as numerals (V=5, I=1, C=100, U/V=5, L=50, D=500, plus multiple I's), sums to 666. Not officially claimed as a papal title today, but historical documents used it.
  2. **[1 Kings 10:14](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.1Kgs10.14):** Solomon received 666 talents of gold annually — a symbol of *human kingdom at its peak* that would corrupt him. The beast may claim to be the pinnacle of God's blessing to humanity.
  3. **Ezra 2:13:** 666 of the clan of Adonikam returned from Babylon. The beast may pretend to be the true remnant returning from exile.
  4. **Anti-Trinitarian symbol:** 6 falling short of 7 (perfection); tripled — the counterfeit trinity of dragon, beast, false prophet.
- **The lesson's caution:** *Do not speculate.* Ronald Reagan's name (6-6-6 letters), Jerusalem bus license plates, and countless other calculations have been proposed and have collapsed. The number's meaning is contested. The *character* of the beast, however, is not. Focus on the character.

## 💡 Deep Discussion Questions

### On the Earth Beast's Identity

1. **Adventist interpretation identifies the earth beast as the United States. This identification was articulated by Ellen White when the U.S. was a fraction of its current global power. What features of the U.S. — historical, political, religious — most clearly correspond to [Revelation 13:11](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.11)?**

2. **The earth beast has "two horns like a lamb" but "speaks like a dragon." This is the tension of a nation that appears Christian but exercises coercive religious power. Where do you see this tension in the U.S. today — in politics, in Christian nationalism, in the culture wars?**

3. **The earth beast arises from the "earth" — sparsely settled territory. This detail seems small but was prophetically significant. What does it mean that God foresaw the geography of the last-days scenario, not merely its politics?**

### On the Coming Religious Coercion

4. **The lesson quotes Ellen White: "the same spirit will be manifested in Protestantism when it obtains control of the civil power" (GC 442). Where in current American religious life do you see this tendency — Protestants seeking civil power to enforce religious convictions?**

5. **Historical persecution has usually come from *within* Christianity, not from secular power. The pattern of Christian-on-Christian religious enforcement is what [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13) predicts. What does this teach about the danger of *any* alliance between religion and coercion?**

6. **Adventist commitment to religious liberty is not incidental to prophetic reading; it is *derived from* prophetic reading. Where in your congregation is religious liberty a serious concern, and where has it faded into background?**

### On Miracles and Discernment

7. **"Fire from heaven" is the sign the earth beast uses to deceive. It echoes Elijah on Carmel — the very sign that authenticated the true God. What does it teach that Satan uses precisely the signs of God to deceive?** How do you not be deceived?

8. **The Adventist doctrine of *conditional immortality* (the dead sleep until resurrection) is a crucial defense against spiritualism. If the dead are not conscious, they cannot appear to us — so any "appearance of the dead" is diabolical impersonation. Have you thought of this doctrine as evangelistic *defense* rather than just abstract belief?**

9. **[Deuteronomy 13:1–5](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Deut13.1) gives the biblical rule: even if a prophet performs miracles and predictions come true, if their doctrine leads away from God, they are false. Where does modern Christian discernment need to recover this principle?**

### On the Image and the Command to Worship

10. **The parallel with [Daniel 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3) is remarkable. Nebuchadnezzar's image, the command to worship, the death penalty for refusal — all reappear in [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13). If you were to live through the antitype, would you recognize the pattern? Have you rehearsed, in imagination, what refusal would cost?**

11. **The image is a *representation* of the beast — a system that replicates the sea beast's methods (church-state union, religious enforcement). What would this look like in a specifically American context? Not identical to medieval Catholicism, but structurally analogous.**

12. **[Gen. 2:7](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Gen2.7) — God breathed life into Adam. [Rev. 13:15](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.15) — the earth beast breathes life into the image. The parallel is a blasphemous imitation. What is the theological significance of Satan trying to *create life*?**

### On the Mark of the Beast

13. **Sunday observance as the mark of the beast — Adventism has taught this for over 150 years. But it is not received *now*, in ordinary Sunday-keeping; it is received *when the crisis comes and Sunday observance is coerced against Sabbath observance*. Have you learned to hold this distinction with care?**

14. **A Sunday-keeping Christian who loves Jesus is not marked with the beast's mark today. But when the crisis comes, the choice will be forced. How do you preach the coming crisis without misrepresenting current sincere Christians?**

15. **The forehead is mind; the hand is work. To be marked in either place is to be marked in loyalty (mind) or action (hand). Where is your own loyalty most tempted to compromise — in what you think, or in what you do?**

### On the Number 666

16. **The lesson explicitly warns against speculation on 666. Yet many have speculated. Ronald Reagan's name, bar codes, credit card chips — the list is long. What is the danger of over-focused number-decoding, and what is the responsible way to hold the mystery?**

17. **The Vicarius Filii Dei calculation is Adventism's traditional reading. It is not the only reading, and it is not definitive. But even if 666 remains uncertain, the *character* of the sea beast is not uncertain. Where should our confidence lie?**

18. **The lesson notes that 666 in [1 Kings 10:14](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.1Kgs10.14) marks Solomon's peak wealth — the point from which his corruption began. Is the beast, through 666, symbolizing *human religion at its glorious peak* — beautiful, powerful, and about to fall?**

## ✍️ Ellen White Integration

### 1. On the union of Protestantism and civil power

> *"When Protestantism shall stretch her hand across the gulf to grasp the hand of the Roman power, when she shall reach over the abyss to clasp hands with spiritualism, when, under the influence of this threefold union, our country shall repudiate every principle of its Constitution as a Protestant and republican government, and shall make provision for the propagation of papal falsehoods and delusions, then we may know that the time has come for the marvelous working of Satan and that the end is near."*
> — **Ellen G. White**, *Testimonies for the Church*, vol. 5, p. 451

**Context:** Ellen White outlines the *threefold union* — Protestantism, Roman power, and spiritualism — that will characterize the final crisis. The three parallel the three unclean spirits of [Rev. 16:13–14](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev16.13). She foresees a specifically American convergence in which the Constitution's principles are quietly abandoned in favor of religious coercion.

**Discussion prompt:** Ellen White foresaw *specific* trends more than a century in advance. Where do you see each of the three — apostate Protestantism, resurgent papal influence, and spiritualism (broadly, the return to occult and paranormal experience) — in current American life?

### 2. On the counterfeit second coming

> *"As the crowning act in the great drama of deception, Satan himself will personate Christ. The church has long professed to look to the Saviour's advent as the consummation of her hopes. Now the great deceiver will make it appear that Christ has come. In different parts of the earth, Satan will manifest himself among men as a majestic being of dazzling brightness, resembling the description of the Son of God given by John in the Revelation."*
> — **Ellen G. White**, *The Great Controversy*, p. 624

**Context:** Ellen White's most explicit teaching on the counterfeit second coming. Satan will impersonate Christ in appearance and demand worship in a form so like the real thing that only those familiar with Scripture's clear description will detect the fraud.

**Discussion prompt:** Ellen White identifies the *only* safeguard as knowing what the *true* Second Coming will look like from Scripture. Do you know the biblical description? Could you distinguish the true from a counterfeit *now*, before the crisis is on?

### 3. On preaching [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13) responsibly

> *"When you have this experience [the love of Christ constraining you], you will not be filled with pride over the position you occupy... You will feel a burden of soul to warn men of danger, to lead them to safety."*
> — **Ellen G. White**, *Testimonies for the Church*, vol. 6, p. 415

**Context:** Ellen White urges that prophetic warning be delivered *with the love of Christ constraining*. The prophetic message is not for scoring points against other Christians; it is for warning those in danger with the tenderness of one who cares deeply.

**Discussion prompt:** How would preaching [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13) sound different if it were delivered by someone who loves Sunday-keeping Christians rather than by someone who wants to prove them wrong? What is the difference in tone, in emphasis, in patience?

## 🛠️ Practical Application

**Individual:**

1. **The [Daniel 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3) rehearsal.** Read [Daniel 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3) slowly, imagining yourself as Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego at the moment the music plays. What will you say to yourself when the day comes? What have you already told yourself for that moment?

2. **The Scripture-vs-phenomenon test.** For a week, when you encounter a Christian teaching, ask: "Does this authenticate itself by *Scripture* or by *phenomenon*?" Notice how much of modern Christian influence rests on experiences rather than exegesis. This is training for the coming days.

3. **Religious liberty as evangelistic practice.** Where in your community can you actively defend religious liberty — for Muslims, for Jews, for Catholics, for atheists — as a witness to the pattern of Christ, who never coerced belief? This is not compromise; it is imitation.

**In Sabbath School:**

1. **The two-beast comparison.** Draw a table: sea beast ([Rev. 13:1–10](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.1)) and earth beast ([Rev. 13:11–18](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13.11)). List their features side by side. What is different? What is the same? What is each beast's role in the final crisis?

2. **The [Daniel 3](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Dan3) – [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13) parallel.** Read both passages side by side. Chart the parallels: image, command, refusal, death threat, God's deliverance. Discuss: what would faithful refusal look like in your specific cultural context?

3. **The religious liberty workshop.** Study the founding of American religious liberty (Roger Williams, the First Amendment, historical Adventist involvement in religious liberty organizations). Discuss: where is religious liberty currently under pressure, and how should the church respond?

**Intellectually:**

1. **Study Ellen White on the U.S. and religious liberty.** *The Great Controversy* chapters 25 ("God's Law Immutable"), 35 ("Liberty of Conscience Threatened"), 36 ("The Impending Conflict"), and 40 ("God's People Delivered") together develop the Adventist eschatological reading of the U.S. Read them slowly.

2. **The counterfeit-Christ deception.** Study the biblical description of the true Second Coming ([Matt. 24:26–31](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Matt24.26); [Acts 1:9–11](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Acts1.9); [1 Thess. 4:16–17](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.1Thess4.16); [2 Thess. 2:8–10](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.2Thess2.8); [Rev. 1:7](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev1.7); [Rev. 19:11–16](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev19.11)) alongside Ellen White's description of the counterfeit (*GC* 624–626). Note the specific differences. Know them cold.

3. **The 666 interpretations, historically surveyed.** Read a scholarly survey of interpretations of 666 across Christian history — Roman emperors (Nero = 666 in Hebrew letters), papal titles, church-state combinations, and more. Understand the range. Then decide what you actually believe, with humility.

## 🧵 Theological Threads

- **The counterfeit trinity:** Dragon (counterfeit Father), sea beast (counterfeit Son with mock death-and-resurrection), earth beast (counterfeit Holy Spirit — the miracle-working, revivalist power that draws worship to another). The threefold structure of Satan's opposition mirrors the Trinity — because Satan cannot innovate; he can only counterfeit.

- **Signs, miracles, and Scripture as arbiter:** Miracles do not authenticate doctrine. Scripture authenticates doctrine. Miracles that lead away from Scripture are, by biblical rule ([Deut. 13:1–5](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Deut13.1)), false regardless of their power. This is the Reformation principle applied to eschatology.

- **The worship crisis:** Behind every political and economic dimension of the final crisis lies the single question: *whom will you worship?* The mark, the image, the number — all resolve to this question.

- **Sabbath as sign of the Creator:** The fourth commandment identifies God as Creator ([Exod. 20:11](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Ex20.11)). To keep the Sabbath is to acknowledge the Creator's authority. To keep Sunday under civil coercion is to acknowledge the beast's authority to modify God's law. The theological weight is the identity of the One being worshiped.

- **Religious liberty as prophetic conviction:** Adventist commitment to religious liberty is not merely cultural preference. It is prophetic. Where religious coercion arises, the beast pattern is emerging. To defend religious liberty is to work against the beast, even when the beast is not yet visibly ascendant.

- **The 144,000 on Mount Zion:** The Memory Text ([Rev. 14:1](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev14.1)) is Revelation's answer to [Revelation 13](https://ref.ly/logosref/Bible.Rev13). Wherever the beast triumphs on earth, the Lamb has already gathered His people on the heavenly Zion. The end of the story is not the beast's; it is the Lamb's.

- **Preaching with tears, not tongs:** Prophetic warning delivered in the spirit of Christ draws people toward safety. Prophetic warning delivered as denunciation drives people into defensiveness. The message is the same; the *heart from which it is spoken* changes everything.
